How it works

Three steps, first one's free.

  1. Fifteen minutes on the phone

    Send a few photos ahead of time, talk through what's frustrating you, find out if it's worth your money before spending any.

  2. We get in the closet

    Two to three hours pulling everything out and building outfits from what you own. You keep photos of every look.

  3. We fill the actual gaps

    Once we know what's missing, I source it — mostly secondhand, within a budget you set first.
